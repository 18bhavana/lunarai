## What to write about

- **Corrections** — found a mistake in a chapter, code that doesn't compile, or an outdated fact? Please include the page link and what's wrong.
- **Topic requests** — tell us what you'd like covered next.
- **Feedback** — what helped, what was confusing, what could be better.
- **Privacy requests** — see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy/) for what data is involved and how to make a request.
- **Business enquiries** — collaborations and advertising.
