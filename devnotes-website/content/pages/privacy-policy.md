## Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected when you use {{siteName}} ({{siteUrl}}), how it is used, and the choices you have. {{siteName}} does not require an account, and we do not ask you for personal information to read the notes.

## Information stored on your device

To make the site work, {{siteName}} saves a few small items in your browser's **local storage**:

- which chapters you have marked as read, and the last chapter you opened;
- your light / dark theme choice;
- whether you have already seen a support message for a topic.

This information stays in your browser on your device. It is not sent to us, and you can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

## Information collected automatically

Like most websites, our hosting provider may automatically record standard technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and the time of the request. This is used to deliver the website, keep it secure and understand overall usage.

The site loads fonts from **Google Fonts**, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## Advertising and cookies

{{siteName}} uses **Google AdSense** to show advertisements. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve and measure ads:

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
- You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting [Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com). You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at [www.aboutads.info/choices](https://www.aboutads.info/choices) or [www.youronlinechoices.eu](https://www.youronlinechoices.eu).
- Learn more about [how Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites).

If you visit from the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, you will be asked for your consent before personalised ads and related cookies are used. You can change your choice at any time using the privacy / consent link shown on the site.

You can also block or delete cookies through your browser settings. If you do, ads will still appear but will be less relevant to you.

## Payments and donations

Contributions made through the [Support page](/support/) are processed entirely by your UPI app and bank. {{siteName}} does not see or store your bank or card details. As with any UPI payment, the recipient receives the payer's name and UPI ID along with the transaction details; these are used only to record the contribution and are never sold or shared.

## Emails you send us

If you contact us by email, we use your email address and message only to reply to you and to fix any issue you report.
